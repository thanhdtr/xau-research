1. HOW REGIME DETECTION WORKS
================================================================================

Every regime detection system answers two questions:
  a) Is the market trending or ranging?    → Use ADX + Autocorrelation
  b) Is volatility normal or elevated?     → Use ATR Ratio

The combination of these two dimensions creates the 4 regimes.

The best-performing systems use a HIERARCHICAL decision tree, not a single
indicator. Decision order matters:

  Step 1: Check volatility FIRST (volatile overrides everything)
  Step 2: Check trend strength SECOND
  Step 3: Classify direction THIRD

This is because a highly volatile market is dangerous regardless of trend.


================================================================================
2. THE 4 REGIMES FOR XAU/USD
================================================================================

+------------------+-----------+------------+----------------------------------+
| Regime           | ADX       | ATR Ratio  | What Gold Does                   |
+------------------+-----------+------------+----------------------------------+
| TRENDING UP      | ADX > 25  | 0.8 - 1.2  | Steady climb, shallow pullbacks  |
| TRENDING DOWN    | ADX > 25  | 0.8 - 1.2  | Steady decline, weak bounces     |
| RANGING          | ADX < 20  | 0.8 - 1.2  | Choppy, bounces off S/R levels   |
| HIGH VOLATILE    | ANY       | > 1.5      | Spikes, whipsaws, news-driven    |
+------------------+-----------+------------+----------------------------------+

Definitions:
  ADX = Average Directional Index (14-period) — measures TREND STRENGTH only
  ATR Ratio = Short ATR(14) / Long ATR(50) — measures volatility expansion

XAU/USD nuances:
  - Gold trends HARDER than most forex pairs (ADX regularly hits 40-60)
  - Gold consolidations are BRUTAL for trend followers (false breakouts)
  - NFP, CPI, FOMC create massive volatility spikes (ATR ratio > 2.0)
  - Gold correlates inversely with USD and with real yields

3. DETECTION ALGORITHM (Step by Step)
================================================================================

--- STEP 1: CALCULATE RAW METRICS ---

  inputs:
    close[]       = closing prices
    high[]        = highs
    low[]         = lows
    lookback      = 14        (for ADX and short ATR)
    long_lookback = 50        (for long ATR)
    lookback_stat = 100       (for autocorrelation)
    vol_threshold = 1.5       (ATR ratio threshold)
    trend_threshold = 25      (ADX threshold)

  a) ATR(14)   = Average True Range over 14 bars
  b) ATR(50)   = Average True Range over 50 bars
  c) ATR Ratio = ATR(14) / ATR(50)
  d) ADX(14)   = Average Directional Index over 14 bars
  e) Slope     = linear regression slope of closes over lookback_stat bars
  f) AutoCorr  = autocorrelation of returns at lag 1 over lookback_stat bars

--- STEP 2: CLASSIFY REGIME (Hierarchical) ---

  if ATR Ratio > vol_threshold:
      regime = HIGH_VOLATILE

  else if ADX > trend_threshold:
      if slope > 0:
          regime = TRENDING_UP
      else:
          regime = TRENDING_DOWN

  else:
      regime = RANGING

--- STEP 3: CONFIRM WITH AUTOCORRELATION (Optional Filter) ---

  autocorrelation adds a statistical confirmation layer:
    - Positive autocorrelation (> 0.1)  → confirms trending
    - Negative autocorrelation (< -0.1) → confirms ranging
    - Near zero (between -0.1 and 0.1) → ambiguous, trust ADX

  This prevents false regime labels during transition periods.

--- STEP 4: SMOOTH TO PREVENT WHIPSAW ---

  Problem: Regime flips every bar cause churning.
  Solution: Require N consecutive bars (default 3) in new regime before switching.

  regime_buffer[0..2] = circular buffer of last 3 regime labels
  if all 3 entries == NEW_REGIME:
      confirmed_regime = NEW_REGIME
  else:
      confirmed_regime = previous_confirmed_regime

4. ADAPTIVE STRATEGY RULES PER REGIME
================================================================================

--- REGIME: TRENDING UP 🚀 ---
  Entry:     Buy on pullback to EMA(21) or EMA(50)
  Filter:    Price must be above EMA(200) on daily
  Stop Loss: 1.5x ATR(14) below entry
  Take Profit: 3x ATR(14) above entry (R:R = 1:2)
  Trailing:  Yes — trail stop at 1x ATR behind price
  Position:  Full size (1-2% risk)
  Exit:      Trailing stop hit, or regime changes away from trending up

--- REGIME: TRENDING DOWN 📉 ---
  Entry:     Sell on bounce to EMA(21) or EMA(50)
  Filter:    Price must be below EMA(200) on daily
  Stop Loss: 1.5x ATR(14) above entry
  Take Profit: 3x ATR(14) below entry (R:R = 1:2)
  Trailing:  Yes — trail stop at 1x ATR above price
  Position:  Full size (1-2% risk)
  Exit:      Trailing stop hit, or regime changes away from trending down

--- REGIME: RANGING 🦀 ---
  Entry:     Buy at support / sell at resistance
  Filter:    RSI(14) < 35 for buys, RSI(14) > 65 for sells
  Stop Loss: Just beyond the range boundary (0.5-1x ATR)
  Take Profit: Opposite side of range (0.6x normal TP multiplier)
  Trailing:  NO — take quick profit at fixed target
  Position:  Reduced size (0.5-1% risk)
  Exit:      TP hit, or regime changes (warning: breakout incoming)

  IMPORTANT: In ranging regime, DISABLE trailing stops.
  Gold ranges are tight and trailing stops get hit by wicks.

--- REGIME: HIGH VOLATILE ⚠️ ---
  Entry:     REDUCE ACTIVITY — only trade A+ setups
  Filter:    Must have confluence of 3+ indicators
  Stop Loss: 2.5x ATR(14) (wider to survive spikes)
  Take Profit: 2x ATR(14) only (reduce targets, take what you can)
  Trailing:  NO — fixed TP only
  Position:  HALF size (0.5% risk max)
  Exit:      TP or SL only, no discretion

  CRITICAL: During volatile regime, many traders sit out entirely.
  The priority is SURVIVAL, not profit.


================================================================================
5. RISK MANAGEMENT OVERLAY (Always Active)
================================================================================

These rules apply in ALL regimes:

  a) Max risk per trade:        1-2% of account
  b) Max daily loss:            3-5% of account (stop trading for the day)
  c) Max concurrent trades:     2-3 positions max
  d) Correlation check:         Don't hold multiple gold trades in same direction
  e) Position sizing formula:
       Position Size = (Account Balance × Risk %) / (Stop Loss in pips × pip value)

  f) Regime-based sizing adjustment:
       TRENDING:   1.0x normal size
       RANGING:    0.6x normal size
       VOLATILE:   0.4x normal size

  g) After 2 consecutive losses: Reduce size by 50% for next 3 trades
  h) After 5 consecutive losses: Stop trading, review regime detection accuracy

6. IMPLEMENTATION (Python Pseudocode)
================================================================================

```python
import numpy as np

# --- CONFIGURATION ---
ADX_PERIOD = 14
ATR_SHORT_PERIOD = 14
ATR_LONG_PERIOD = 50
LOOKBACK_STAT = 100
VOL_THRESHOLD = 1.5
TREND_THRESHOLD = 25
CONFIRM_BARS = 3

# --- INDICATORS ---
def calc_atr(high, low, close, period):
    """Average True Range"""
    tr = np.maximum(high - low,
         np.maximum(abs(high - np.roll(close, 1)),
                    abs(low - np.roll(close, 1))))
    return np.convolve(tr, np.ones(period)/period, mode='valid')

def calc_adx(high, low, close, period):
    """Average Directional Index (simplified)"""
    plus_dm = np.maximum(np.diff(high), 0)
    minus_dm = np.maximum(-np.diff(low), 0)
    # Mask: only keep the larger of the two
    mask = plus_dm > minus_dm
    plus_dm[~mask] = 0
    minus_dm[mask] = 0
    atr = calc_atr(high, low, close, period)
    plus_di = 100 * np.convolve(plus_dm, np.ones(period)/period, mode='valid') / atr
    minus_di = 100 * np.convolve(minus_dm, np.ones(period)/period, mode='valid') / atr
    dx = 100 * abs(plus_di - minus_di) / (plus_di + minus_di + 1e-10)
    adx = np.convolve(dx, np.ones(period)/period, mode='valid')
    return adx

def calc_slope(close, lookback):
    """Linear regression slope of closes"""
    x = np.arange(lookback)
    slope = np.polyfit(x, close[-lookback:], 1)[0]
    return slope

def calc_autocorrelation(returns, lag=1):
    """Autocorrelation of returns at given lag"""
    if len(returns) < lag + 2:
        return 0
    x = returns[:-lag]
    y = returns[lag:]
    if np.std(x) == 0 or np.std(y) == 0:
        return 0
    return np.corrcoef(x, y)[0, 1]

# --- REGIME DETECTOR ---
class RegimeDetector:
    def __init__(self):
        self.regime_buffer = []
        self.confirmed_regime = "RANGING"  # default

    def detect(self, closes, highs, lows):
        """Returns confirmed regime string"""
        # Step 1: Calculate metrics
        atr_short = calc_atr(highs, lows, closes, ATR_SHORT_PERIOD)[-1]
        atr_long = calc_atr(highs, lows, closes, ATR_LONG_PERIOD)[-1]
        atr_ratio = atr_short / atr_long if atr_long > 0 else 1.0
        adx = calc_adx(highs, lows, closes, ADX_PERIOD)[-1]
        slope = calc_slope(closes, LOOKBACK_STAT)
        returns = np.diff(np.log(closes[-LOOKBACK_STAT:]))
        autocorr = calc_autocorrelation(returns)

        # Step 2: Classify (hierarchical — volatile first)
        if atr_ratio > VOL_THRESHOLD:
            raw_regime = "HIGH_VOLATILE"
        elif adx > TREND_THRESHOLD:
            raw_regime = "TRENDING_UP" if slope > 0 else "TRENDING_DOWN"
        else:
            raw_regime = "RANGING"

        # Step 3: Confirm with autocorrelation (soft filter)
        if raw_regime == "TRENDING_UP" and autocorr < -0.1:
            raw_regime = "RANGING"  # autocorrelation contradicts
        elif raw_regime == "TRENDING_DOWN" and autocorr < -0.1:
            raw_regime = "RANGING"
        elif raw_regime == "RANGING" and autocorr > 0.2:
            raw_regime = "TRENDING_UP" if slope > 0 else "TRENDING_DOWN"

        # Step 4: Smooth — require CONFIRM_BARS consecutive
        self.regime_buffer.append(raw_regime)
        if len(self.regime_buffer) > CONFIRM_BARS:
            self.regime_buffer.pop(0)

        if len(self.regime_buffer) == CONFIRM_BARS:
            if len(set(self.regime_buffer)) == 1:
                self.confirmed_regime = self.regime_buffer[0]

        return self.confirmed_regime, {
            "adx": round(adx, 1),
            "atr_ratio": round(atr_ratio, 2),
            "slope": round(slope, 6),
            "autocorr": round(autocorr, 3),
        }

# --- STRATEGY ENGINE ---
def get_strategy(regime, account_balance, risk_pct=0.01):
    """Returns strategy parameters for the current regime"""
    strategies = {
        "TRENDING_UP": {
            "direction": "BUY_ONLY",
            "entry": "pullback_to_ema21",
            "sl_atr_mult": 1.5,
            "tp_atr_mult": 3.0,
            "trailing": True,
            "size_multiplier": 1.0,
            "risk_pct": risk_pct,
        },
        "TRENDING_DOWN": {
            "direction": "SELL_ONLY",
            "entry": "bounce_to_ema21",
            "sl_atr_mult": 1.5,
            "tp_atr_mult": 3.0,
            "trailing": True,
            "size_multiplier": 1.0,
            "risk_pct": risk_pct,
        },
        "RANGING": {
            "direction": "BOTH",
            "entry": "buy_support_sell_resistance",
            "sl_atr_mult": 0.8,
            "tp_atr_mult": 1.5,   # reduced targets
            "trailing": False,
            "size_multiplier": 0.6,
            "risk_pct": risk_pct * 0.6,
        },
        "HIGH_VOLATILE": {
            "direction": "A_PLUS_ONLY",
            "entry": "triple_confluence",
            "sl_atr_mult": 2.5,   # wider stops
            "tp_atr_mult": 2.0,   # reduced targets
            "trailing": False,
            "size_multiplier": 0.4,
            "risk_pct": risk_pct * 0.4,
        },
    }
    return strategies.get(regime, strategies["RANGING"])
```

7. XAU/USD-SPECIFIC TUNING
================================================================================

Gold has unique behaviors that require adjusting the defaults:

  a) TIMEFRAME: Use H1 or H4 for regime detection (M15 is too noisy for ADX)
     - H1: faster regime changes, more signals, more whipsaws
     - H4: smoother regime detection, fewer false signals
     - D1: too slow — gold regime shifts happen over days, not weeks

  b) ADX THRESHOLD: Gold trends harder than EUR/USD
     - Default ADX threshold: 25
     - For gold, consider raising to 28-30 to avoid false trend signals
     - Gold ADX regularly reaches 50-70 during strong moves

  c) ATR RATIO THRESHOLD: Gold volatility is inherently higher
     - Default vol threshold: 1.5
     - For gold, consider 1.6-1.7 (gold's baseline ATR is wider)

  d) SEASONAL PATTERNS: Gold has known seasonal tendencies
     - Strong: Jan-Feb (January effect), Aug-Sep (jewelry demand)
     - Weak: Jun-Jul (summer doldrums)
     - Regime detection can be weighted by seasonal tendency

  e) NEWS EVENTS: Force HIGH_VOLATILE regime during:
     - NFP (first Friday of month)
     - CPI data releases
     - FOMC decisions
     - Geopolitical crises
     - Don't wait for ATR to spike — pre-empt with a calendar overlay

  f) CORRELATION FILTER:
     - Check DXY (Dollar Index) — strong inverse correlation
     - Check US 10Y yield — gold falls when yields rise
     - If DXY is trending strongly, gold regime is likely opposite
